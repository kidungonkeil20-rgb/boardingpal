Delete your BoardingPal account

Operated by Cosner Pagaddut

You don't need the app to do this. You can also delete your account in the app: Profile → Settings & privacy → Delete account.

What happens when you delete your account

  • Your profile, photos, saved listings, notifications and device tokens are permanently deleted.
  • Owners: all your listings, rooms and listing photos are deleted.
  • Your pending and approved reservations are cancelled, and the other party is notified.
  • Past reservation records stay with the other party with your name and messages removed, and are erased within 2 years.
  • Reports you filed are kept for up to 1 year after they close, without your identity, for safety follow-up.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Details: Privacy Policy, section 7.

Confirm it's you

We'll email a 6-digit code to this address if it has a BoardingPal account.

Lost access to your email?

Email our privacy contact with the subject "Account deletion request". Include your name, whether you were a student or an owner, and details only the account holder would know (for example, a listing name or a recent reservation date).

We'll never ask for your password. We verify the request, delete the account within 30 days, and confirm by email.
